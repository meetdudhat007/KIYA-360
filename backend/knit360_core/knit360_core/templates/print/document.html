{# The shared body of every KNIT 360 print format -- FR-SALES-002 / FR-FIN-003. One file, included by each format, because a quotation and the invoice that follows it must look like they came from the same company. Six copies of this layout would be six places for the column widths to drift apart. A wrapper sets these and includes this file: heading what the document is called in capitals, e.g. QUOTATION party_label "To" or "Supplier" party_name who it is addressed to party_lines further lines under the name (address, contact) facts [(label, value)] for the block on the right columns [{label, key, align, money}] for the line table rows the line items totals [(label, amount, strong)] under the table terms free text printed at the foot, or nothing closing a final sentence, or nothing Deliberately plain: black on white, one serif face, rules rather than panels, no colour and no icons. A printed invoice is a legal record of a transaction, and it should read like one. #}
