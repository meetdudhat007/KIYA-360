### Operating Constraints
This document maps KIYA business entities across all 28 modules to ERPNext DocTypes or Frappe Framework concepts based strictly on documented evidence. 
- Mapping an entity does **NOT** mean ERPNext's schema is approved as KIYA's data model.
- Mapping must not force 1-to-1 relationships where structural discrepancies exist.
- Concepts provided by Frappe Framework (outside the ERPNext repository) are explicitly segregated from ERPNext DocTypes.
- All unresolved fields, lifecycles, and validation rules remain `TBD` under `OQ-002`.

## 2. Relationship Types and Match Quality Standards

| Relationship Type | Description | Match Quality Standard |
|---|---|---|
| **1:1 Exact Equivalent** | A direct conceptual and structural mapping between a single KIYA entity and a single ERPNext DocType. | **High / Exact** |
| **1:1 Partial Equivalent** | High-level business concepts align, but substantial field, status, or lifecycle differences exist. | **Medium / Partial** |
| **Composite Mapping (1:N or N:1)** | A single KIYA entity maps to multiple ERPNext DocTypes, or multiple KIYA concepts are unified into one ERPNext record. | **Medium / Composite** |
| **Analogy Only** | Superficial or partial functional resemblance; fundamental purpose or operational mechanics differ. | **Low / Analogous** |
| **KIYA-Only Concept** | Explicitly required by KIYA BRD, but completely absent from ERPNext and Frappe Framework. | **Missing / Build-Required** |
| **ERPNext-Only Concept** | Exists in ERPNext as an internal operational or caching mechanism; not required as a primary KIYA business concept. | **ERPNext-Internal** |
| **Frappe-Framework Concept** | Provided by Frappe Framework core, not part of the ERPNext business application repository. | **Framework-Owned** |

## 4. Synthesis of Domain Mapping Patterns

The domain mapping establishes five architectural categories:

1. **Direct Core Equivalents (High Reuse Candidates):** 
   `Company`, `Customer`, `Supplier`, `Item`, `Opportunity`, `Quotation`, `Sales Order`, `Purchase Order`, `Delivery Note`, `Purchase Receipt`, `Sales Invoice`, `Purchase Invoice`, `Payment Entry`, `GL Entry`, `Stock Ledger Entry`, `BOM`, `Work Order (Mfg)`, `Job Card`, `Project`, `Task`, `Timesheet`.
2. **Missing KIYA-Mandated Entities (Mandatory KIYA Build):**
   - `Enquiry` (Sales/CRM)
   - `RFP` (Procurement)
   - `Field Service Work Order` (Maintenance & Field Service)
   - `Customer Installed Asset` (Asset Management)
   - `WMS Physical Location / Bin` (Warehouse)
   - `India GST Master Set` (Tax & Compliance)
   - `AI / ML Governance & Insight Records` (AI & Automation)
   - `Enterprise DMS Repository` (Document Management)
3. **Framework-Owned Entities (Frappe Core):**
   `User`, `Role`, `User Permission`, `File`, `Workflow`, `Notification`, `Version`, `Activity Log`.
4. **Structural & Semantic Collisions:**
   - **Bin:** ERPNext Bin is an item-balance cache; KIYA Bin is a physical storage location.
   - **Work Order:** ERPNext Work Order is factory manufacturing only; KIYA requires both manufacturing and field service work orders.
   - **Asset:** ERPNext Asset is internal capitalized property; KIYA Asset-to-Service requires customer equipment management.
5. **Decoupled Quality Pipeline:**
   `Quality Inspection` (Stock) and `Non Conformance` (Quality Management) exist as separate entities with no automated transition logic in the core repository.
