### Scope & Anti-Hallucination Standards
This document assesses the coverage of the analyzed ERPNext reference repository (`references/erpnext-develop/`, v17.0.0-dev) against all **28 KIYA BRD modules** and their 238 functional requirements.
- **ERPNext Absence vs Frappe Absence:** We distinguish between capabilities absent from ERPNext's business application logic versus capabilities provided by the underlying Frappe Framework (e.g., auth, permissions, basic file storage, REST API).
- **No Invented Requirements:** Gaps are strictly identified against explicit BRD requirements. Where BRD details are unspecified, gaps are classified as `TBD` under open questions.
- **Coverage Levels:** `Full`, `Partial`, `Weak`, `Missing`, `Framework-Provided`.

---

## 2. Coverage Level Definitions

| Coverage Level | Meaning | Architectural Impact on KIYA |
|---|---|---|
| **Full** | ERPNext provides mature, out-of-the-box DocTypes and business logic that satisfy the baseline BRD requirement without major structural additions. | Direct candidate for core configuration/reuse. |
| **Partial** | Core business logic exists, but specific workflows, sub-entities, advanced features, or KIYA-mandated sequences are omitted. | Requires custom extension app, hooks, or supplementary logic. |
| **Weak** | ERPNext provides basic data fields or simplistic reporting, but lacks the operational depth, automation, or enterprise rigor required by the BRD. | Substantial custom engineering or external component required. |
| **Missing** | Completely absent from both ERPNext and Frappe Framework. | Must be 100% KIYA-owned custom build or integrated third-party service. |
| **Framework-Provided** | Not in ERPNext repository, but natively supplied by Frappe Framework core (outside the reference tree). | Dependent on Frappe Framework adoption and operational ownership. |

## 4. Synthesis of High-Risk Deficiencies

Across the 28 modules, the gap analysis highlights nine critical risk clusters where ERPNext provides little or no coverage:

1. **HR & Payroll (Module 19):** Completely absent from the ERPNext develop tree; core HRMS was extracted to an external app.
2. **AI & Automation (Module 25):** 100% missing from the codebase.
3. **India Tax & GST Statutory Compliance (Module 18):** Localization explicitly deleted via migration patches (`remove_india_localisation.py`).
4. **Physical Warehouse Management (Module 09):** ERPNext `Bin` is an accounting balance record, not an aisle/shelf physical location.
5. **Customer Installed Base & Field Service (Modules 13 & 14):** Inability to manage customer equipment, technician geo-dispatch, and truck-stock spares; severe naming collision on "Work Order".
6. **Enterprise Performance Management (Module 23):** Budget checks exist only as basic GL account spend caps; no multi-scenario financial planning.
7. **Native Mobile Offline Architecture (Module 27):** Frappe is web-first; native offline synchronization does not exist.
8. **Enterprise Document Management (Module 21):** Simple file storage exists, but no OCR, check-in/out, or legal retention.
9. **SaaS Multi-Tenant Control Plane (Module 01):** Frappe supports site-per-tenant, but has no multi-tenant billing, subscription management, or automated provisioning control plane in the repository.
